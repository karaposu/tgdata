## Risk Review

No additional severity finding is warranted by the actual interfaces after tracing the
plan. Four subtle choices are explicit and must remain so during implementation:

- Recognition precedes fresh clock/counter checks, but the conservative local-busy
  refusal remains in force. An old recognized result reports facts and never clears a
  newer attempt; unrecognized stale commands cannot rebase their expected control.
- Recovery time is the trusted observation made after quiescence and saved atomically
  with acceptance. It is not a claim to know the backend's physical commit timestamp.
  Time spent awaiting CAS is real quiet time after the caller asserted stoppage; a lost
  reply must not restart that interval. New-method tests should make write latency visible.
- In-memory end evidence may survive a failed publication/cancellation in the same
  engine. After losing that evidence, conservative recovery must use a fresh full
  interval. Candidate fixtures cannot prove the cache is keyed correctly; Step 5 does.
- Source wait hints are intentionally observations. Unknown/indefinite values stay
  None, and even a zero local wait is not a guarantee of current budget/server permission.

Wire compatibility remains within the existing record keys; additive internal result
fields have defaults and explicit validation. Resource cost is two small timing entries
per touched group and the existing one-record CAS; no scheduler, lease or general clock
framework is justified. Local quiescence is deliberately stricter than detecting only
an in-flight raw callback, covering admission/publication awaits without a new task owner.
