# Inquiry archive

The unsuffixed discipline files are the final pass (iteration3); Articulate-Simple
was performed once and supplies the original framing. Files ending `_iter1.md` or
`_iter2.md` preserve the earlier completed passes. Superseded route maps are also
archived; the final route map and persistent concept index remain in the inquiry root.

Historical text references are preserved. In each snapshot, an unqualified canonical
discipline name refers to that snapshot's own iteration; use the corresponding suffix
when reading it here. Evidence and framing paths were written relative to the inquiry
root. The final finding supplies current clickable links to the key evidence.
